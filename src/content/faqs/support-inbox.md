The Support page collects the messages members send from the help desk and feedback forms in the Mila apps.

## Who can use it

Stewards and Moderators can both open Support, read every message and mark messages resolved.

## Where the messages come from

Members send a message in one of two ways, and both end up here:

- On the member site: the **Studio Help Desk** and **Send Feedback** links on the sign-in page.
- In the mobile app: the **Help & feedback** screen (from the sign-in screen or Settings), where the member picks Get help or Send feedback.
- Either way the message can be up to 2,000 characters, the member solves a captcha, and one network address can send at most 3 messages per 15 minutes.

The member sees a confirmation. On the member site a help request says "Someone from the Mila team will look into it shortly." In the mobile app every message ends with "We answer by email." That promise cannot be kept, because no email address is stored with the message.

> **Important:** A message holds only its type, its text and the time it was sent. It does not hold the member's name, email or account. You cannot see who sent it, and the admin app has no way to reply. The only way to answer someone is if they wrote their own contact details inside the message.

Because of this, treat every message as anonymous. Do not try to work out who wrote it by searching other pages. A message can still contain personal details the member typed, so do not screenshot or share messages. The privacy rules in [Database browser](/faqs/database-browser) apply here too.

## What the page shows

Two tabs, each with its own count and its own search box.

| Tab | Holds | Search box says |
|---|---|---|
| Help Desk | Messages sent as "help" | Search help desk messages |
| Feedback | Messages sent as "feedback" | Search feedback |

Each tab is a table with three columns:

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Message | The text. A resolved message is greyed out with a line through it |
| Received | The date and time, in your own time zone |
| Status | A small circle button. An empty circle means open, a green tick means resolved |

Search looks inside the message text only and ignores capital letters. Click the Message or Received heading to sort. The page loads the 200 newest messages, resolved ones included.

Empty tabs say "No help desk messages." and "No feedback yet."

## Mark a message resolved

1. Read the message.
2. Deal with it by whatever means fits. For a bug or a gap, tell the owner. See [Known issues and owner actions](/faqs/known-issues-and-owner-actions).
3. Click the circle in the Status column. The text goes grey with a line through it and the circle becomes a green tick.

There is no confirmation and no note field. Hovering over the button shows "Mark resolved" or "Mark unresolved".

## Reopen a message

Click the green tick. The message returns to normal. Anyone with access to Support can do this.

## What changes for the member

Nothing. The member never sees the status of their message, and marking it resolved sends them nothing.

## What the numbers mean

- On the Dashboard, the **Open Support Messages** card counts messages that are not resolved, across both tabs. It opens this page.
- The tab counts on this page include resolved messages, so they only go down when old messages fall outside the newest 200.

## Everything is recorded

Resolving and reopening each write a line to the staff audit log with your account and which message it was (its id, not its text).

## Common questions

**Can I reply to a member from here?** No. There is no reply button and no address to reply to.

**A message asks for help with an account or a refund. What do I do?** If it has no contact details, you cannot answer it directly. Look at the topic and see whether the answer belongs in an article, such as [Credits and refunds](/faqs/credits-and-refunds) or [Sign-in and sessions](/faqs/sign-in-and-sessions), and tell the owner when a question keeps coming up.

**The member site promised someone would look into it, and the mobile app says "We answer by email." Is that true?** The messages do arrive here and staff can read them. But neither form asks for contact details and the admin app cannot send replies, so a reply is only possible if the member typed her own details into the message. Tell the owner when members expect an email answer.

**Is there a filter for open messages only?** Not on this page. Sort by Received and look for messages that are not greyed out.

**Why is the status of this article "partly live"?** The inbox works, but the missing reply route is a known gap. Collecting a contact address with each message is not built yet.
